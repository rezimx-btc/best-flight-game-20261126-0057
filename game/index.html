SKYLINE COURIER

Three missions. One little cargo plane. Take off from the runway, thread every checkpoint ring, dodge birds and storm cells, watch your fuel — and land soft on the strip to deliver the parcels. Land too hard and the cargo is scrap.

How to play: hold → / D to build speed, then pitch up with ↑ / W to lift off · fly through the glowing rings (dead centre = PERFECT) · ease off the throttle and flare with Space to land gently inside the strip · chain rings without a miss to raise your COMBO (up to ×3) · running dry means a glide — pick your landing.
